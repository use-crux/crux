Command center · founder first

What should Crux do next?

Crux has built a large technical surface before earning distribution. The next phase is not “build more.” It is to discover a narrow, expensive problem, help real teams solve it, and turn the repeated parts into a business.

Version 1.012 July 2026Operating horizon: 24 monthsConfidence: exploratory
Working thesis: earn adoption today through reliable, inspectable AI harnesses; learn from production workflows; build certified specialist-model infrastructure only after customers repeatedly ask for it and can supply usable evidence.

The honest diagnosis

What is strong
  • A coherent harness-engineering thesis.
  • Source-linked Project Index, runtime evidence, Quality suites, cassettes, baselines, routing, memory, retrieval, and safety primitives.
  • Provider-neutral architecture and local-first credibility.
  • A plausible long-term bridge from evidence to post-training.
What is missing
  • People who know Crux exists.
  • A five-minute first success that is impossible to misunderstand.
  • A narrow ideal customer and repeated use case.
  • External proof that the system saves money, prevents failure, or increases revenue.
Recommendation

Operate as a problem-discovery company for 90 days

Keep the OSS core moving only where it improves activation or a live design-partner outcome. Spend the rest of the founder’s attention on conversations, hands-on pilots, and public proof. Implementation capacity is abundant; attention and credible evidence are scarce.

The next three moves

1. Make one reliability proof undeniable

Wrap an existing SDK call, expose why it failed, protect it with a baseline, then demonstrate a cheaper model passing the same bar. This ties today’s product to tomorrow’s thesis without building training.

2. Conduct ten problem interviews

Find teams with production AI, repeated failures, measurable outcomes, and meaningful model or review spend. Do not begin by pitching “context engineering.”

3. Recruit one design partner

Offer a fixed-scope reliability sprint around one workflow. Deliver a before/after evidence report, a protected baseline, and a recommendation on whether specialization is economically justified.

Decision gate

Do not build training orchestration yet

Proceed toward governed dataset export only after at least three real teams provide suitable production evidence, experience the reliability workflow, and explicitly want model specialization. Proceed toward one-shot training only after at least one customer will pay for the outcome.
