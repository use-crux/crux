Execute · roadmap

Build only after the evidence arrives

This roadmap is organized by what Crux must learn, not by how many features an agent can implement. Every phase has an entry condition, commercial experiment, product work, and explicit permission to stop.

Start: July 2026Horizon: 24 monthsCadence: monthly review
Roadmap rule: shipping faster does not let Crux skip discovery, distribution, customer trust, or statistically honest proof. Automation compresses build time; it does not manufacture demand.

Phase 0 — now to 30 days

Objective

Make the current value legible

  • Test installation and activation with three unfamiliar developers.
  • Choose and record one flagship reliability proof.
  • Clarify one front-door message and one action.
  • Build a list of 25 candidate teams; conduct the first five interviews.
Evidence to exit

Someone reaches value

  • Three successful observed activations.
  • Time to first useful evidence under 20 minutes.
  • Five interviews with documented workflows.
  • At least two credible design-partner candidates.
Gate 0

If strangers cannot activate, stop expanding the product

Fix installation, vocabulary, examples, error messages, and the demo until they can. Stars, page views, and compliments do not substitute.

Phase 1 — 30 to 90 days

Objective

Deliver the first outcome

  • Run one or two tightly scoped design partnerships.
  • Instrument a real production workflow.
  • Create a failure taxonomy and protected baseline.
  • Compare a meaningful harness or model change.
  • Publish an anonymized or named case study.
Evidence to exit

The workflow repeats

  • One quantified before/after result.
  • Weekly use by at least one external team.
  • Three teams report substantially the same pain.
  • One partner refers another or asks to continue.
Gate 1

If every engagement is unique, do not build a platform

Narrow the segment or workflow. A pile of unrelated custom projects is evidence that the category is too broad, not that the product needs more primitives.

Phase 2 — 3 to 6 months

Objective

Charge and productize

  • Sell the first fixed-price Reliability Sprint.
  • Turn repeated analysis into a stable report or local workflow.
  • Improve shared review, evidence selection, and certification only where repeated.
  • Create the portable learning-package prototype manually.
  • Test pricing and case-study terms.
Evidence to exit

Value survives payment

  • Two paid pilots or one paid renewal.
  • At least €15k cumulative customer revenue.
  • One repeatable deliverable with bounded founder time.
  • A clear buyer and measurable value metric.
Gate 2

If teams use Crux but will not pay, separate adoption from business

Test whether payment belongs to support, hosted coordination, evaluation volume, or a different buyer. Do not hide weak willingness to pay behind free OSS growth.

Phase 3 — 6 to 12 months

Objective

Choose the actual company

  • Decide whether Crux is primarily OSS + support, an AI reliability product, or a specialization control plane.
  • Build the smallest shared service required by retained customers.
  • Add governed dataset export if production evidence repeatedly needs it.
  • Run one provider-delegated specialization experiment if a customer funds it.
  • Establish support limits and recurring pricing.
Evidence to exit

Recurring pull exists

  • Three to five retained production workflows.
  • At least €3k MRR or consistent paid engagements.
  • Two quantified public proofs.
  • One repeated request that existing providers do not solve.
Gate 3

Specialization requires paid pull

Do not implement provider orchestration unless one customer will pay for the package and three workflows have sufficient eligible evidence and independent evals.

Phase 4 — 12 to 24 months

Conditional objective

Operate certified Specialists

  • Formalize the Specialist task contract.
  • Offer method selection across harness, routing, retrieval, SFT, preference tuning, and RFT.
  • Delegate training and serving through customer-chosen providers.
  • Maintain certification, canary, fallback, rollback, and lineage.
  • Explore VPC/on-prem only with enterprise demand.
Success evidence

The specialist is the product

  • Five or more active certified Specialists.
  • Measured quality at or above protected baseline.
  • Material cost, latency, privacy, or revenue value.
  • Recurring payment tied to operation rather than one training job.

Immediate sequence

Activation → interviews → design partner → paid sprint → repeated product → specialization

Never reverse this sequence merely because implementation is fast. Each arrow represents a different kind of evidence.

Founder action

Create a monthly decision review

On the first working day of each month, update the scorecard, assumption ledger, customer evidence, and next gate. Delete roadmap items that no longer serve the current gate.
