---
title: EVIDENCE_WRITE_QUARANTINED
description: A closed Eval cell rejected a late evidence authoring call.
---

## What failed

`evidence.record()` reached the admission gate after its owning Eval cell had
closed.

## Retryability

Not retryable. The closed cell cannot accept another write.

No evidence ID, sequence, span, collector row, or graph record is consumed.

## Fix

Await the producer or record its evidence before the Eval deadline. Review the
Eval diagnostic for the late task rather than suppressing the rejection.
