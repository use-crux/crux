---
title: EVIDENCE_SUPERSESSION_INVALID
description: Evidence supersession is duplicated, cyclic, or identity-incompatible.
---

## What failed

A successor referenced itself, repeated a predecessor, formed a cycle, or
targeted evidence from another subject or role.

## Retryability

Not retryable. The supersession set must be corrected.

Diagnostics do not expose the prior evidence graph.

## Fix

Supersede unique earlier refs for the same subject and role. Keep unrelated or
contradictory claims active instead of using timestamps as replacement order.
