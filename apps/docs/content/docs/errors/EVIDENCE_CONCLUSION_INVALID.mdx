---
title: EVIDENCE_CONCLUSION_INVALID
description: The evidence conclusion does not belong to its selected role.
---

## What failed

The conclusion is outside the closed vocabulary for the relationship role.
Intent evidence never accepts a conclusion.

## Retryability

Not retryable. The authored conclusion must change.

The diagnostic reports only the code and safe remediation.

## Fix

Use one conclusion documented for `authority`, `change`, `verification`, or
`recovery`, or omit it when the producer has no conclusion.
