---
title: EVIDENCE_STAGING_CAPACITY
description: Durable Local evidence staging is temporarily at capacity.
---

## What failed

An artifact arrived before its relationship and could not fit within the
per-evidence, namespace, or project staging quota.

## Retryability

Retryable. Core delivery owns bounded backoff and retry.

The disposition and aggregate health signal contain no payload, subject,
evidence ID, or private staging digest.

## Fix

Allow delivery to retry after candidates resolve or expire. Investigate a
sustained signal as stalled or heavily reordered evidence delivery.
