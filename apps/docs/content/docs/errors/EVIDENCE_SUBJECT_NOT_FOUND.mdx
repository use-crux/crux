---
title: EVIDENCE_SUBJECT_NOT_FOUND
description: The readable destination cannot resolve the requested evidence subject.
---

## What failed

The destination has no authorized, retained canonical identity for the subject.

## Retryability

Not retryable. The current subject identity cannot be resolved.

The response does not distinguish an unknown subject from an unauthorized one.

## Fix

Use the exact run, span, or artifact identity returned by Crux Local. Confirm
the relationship has not expired or been privacy-deleted.
