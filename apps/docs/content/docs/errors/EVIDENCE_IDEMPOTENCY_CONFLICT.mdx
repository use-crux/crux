---
title: EVIDENCE_IDEMPOTENCY_CONFLICT
description: Content diverges from the first accepted evidence identity.
---

## What failed

The same durable evidence or protected approval occurrence identity was reused
with different final post-policy content.

## Retryability

Not retryable. The first accepted content remains immutable for that delivery.

Diagnostics may include a bounded evidence ID correlation, but never the
winning digest, payload, subject, or raw idempotency key.

## Fix

Retry the exact original relationship, or use a new stable occurrence identity
for a genuinely different claim. Inspect delivery health or `observe.flush()`
for asynchronous Local conflicts.
