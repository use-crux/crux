---
title: DEFER_SCOPE_SEALED
description: defer() registration happened after the invocation was sealed.
---

## What failed

`defer()` was called after the execution scope sealed registration (the host
already finalized the logical outcome and handed drain control to the lifetime).

## Why

Sealing linearizes registration versus response commitment. Late registration
would race the host response and bounds.

## What still works

Callbacks already registered continue to drain under host limits. Nested
registrations during an active drain wave remain allowed until nesting/count
limits close them.

## Fix

Register deferred work during the handler body (or nested callbacks while the
drain wave is open), not after the response path has sealed the scope.
