---
title: EVIDENCE_REFERENCE_INVALID
description: An evidence subject or source is not a canonical graph reference.
---

## What failed

The execution, artifact, or reserved effect-receipt reference is malformed or
cannot be projected safely.

## Retryability

Not retryable. The reference must be corrected.

The diagnostic never echoes raw resource IDs or paths.

## Fix

Use exact Crux-issued execution, artifact, and effect-receipt identities. Keep
the receipt returned by an effect definition's `.run()` method instead of
constructing or editing the reference.
